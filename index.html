How planning works

Build your Vacation / Trip and Big Purchase plans first — those planner totals automatically become your linked goals. If you expect a bonus or other windfall, define it on the Forecast page. Then use Goals to optionally decide how much of that windfall you want to put toward each goal. You do not need a windfall to use the planners or goals.

Goals

Vacation and big-purchase targets come from their planners. Their progress shows what has actually been paid.
